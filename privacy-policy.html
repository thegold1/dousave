抖保存 隐私权政策

适用应用：抖保存（软件包名 com.doubaocun.app）
开发者：Crazy Mice
生效日期：2026 年 11 月 23 日

本政策说明「抖保存」（以下简称「本应用」）如何处理您的信息。简而言之：本应用不要求注册登录，不收集、不存储、不出售、也不与任何人共享您的个人数据。所有处理都在您的设备上完成。

1. 本应用做什么

当您把短视频的分享链接交给本应用（通过系统「分享」、粘贴，或在您开启相关功能后自动识别复制的链接），本应用会访问该链接对应的公开网页，解析出视频地址，并把视频下载保存到您手机的相册中。

2. 我们处理哪些信息

  • 您提供的链接：仅在您的设备上用于解析和下载对应视频，不会上传到开发者的任何服务器。
  • 剪贴板内容：仅在您主动粘贴，或您开启「复制链接自动下载」功能后、在视频平台中点击「复制链接」时读取，且只用于识别其中的视频链接。剪贴板内容不会被保存或发送给开发者。
  • 下载的视频：保存在您设备的相册（「抖保存」相册）中，由您自行管理和删除。

本应用没有开发者自己的服务器，不收集设备标识符、位置、通讯录、账号信息或使用统计数据，不包含任何第三方统计或广告 SDK。

3. 无障碍服务的使用（如适用）

如果您在系统设置中为本应用开启了无障碍服务，本应用仅监听视频平台 App 中「复制链接」按钮的点击事件，以便在您复制链接后自动开始下载，并在屏幕上显示下载进度提示。本应用不会读取、记录或上传您在其他 App 中的屏幕内容、输入内容或任何个人信息。您可以随时在系统「设置 → 无障碍」中关闭该服务。

4. 网络请求与第三方

为了下载视频，本应用会直接连接视频所在平台的公开网页和视频服务器。这些请求与您用浏览器打开该链接相同，对方服务器可能按其自身隐私政策记录 IP 地址等常规网络信息。本应用与这些平台没有任何关联，也不向其提供额外数据。

5. 权限说明

  • 网络访问：用于解析链接和下载视频。
  • 写入存储（仅 Android 9 及以下）：用于把视频保存到相册。
  • 无障碍服务（可选）：见第 3 条，默认关闭，需要您手动开启。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的任何个人信息。

7. 内容与版权

请仅下载您拥有权利或已获授权保存的内容，并遵守相关平台的用户协议及当地法律。本应用与抖音、字节跳动或任何视频平台均无关联。

8. 政策变更

如本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页面并修改上方的生效日期。若将来加入广告或其他会处理个人数据的功能，我们会在相关功能上线前更新本政策。

DouSave Privacy Policy

App: DouSave / 抖保存 (package com.doubaocun.app)
Developer: Crazy Mice
Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how DouSave ("the app") handles your information. In short: the app requires no account and does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. All processing happens on your device.

1. What the app does

When you give the app a short-video share link (via the system Share menu, by pasting it, or — if you enable it — by copying a link), the app opens the public web page for that link, finds the video address and saves the video to your phone's gallery.

2. Information we process

  • Links you provide are used only on your device to find and download the video. They are never sent to any server run by the developer.
  • Clipboard content is read only when you paste, or — if you turn on automatic download — when you tap "Copy link" in a video app, and only to detect a video link. It is not stored or sent to the developer.
  • Downloaded videos are saved in your device gallery (the "抖保存" album) and are fully under your control.

The app has no developer-operated server and does not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, account information or usage analytics. It contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

3. Accessibility service (if enabled)

If you enable the app's accessibility service in system settings, the app only listens for taps on the "Copy link" button inside video apps, so it can start the download automatically and show a progress message on screen. It does not read, record or upload screen content, typed text or any personal information from other apps. You can turn the service off at any time in Settings → Accessibility.

4. Network requests and third parties

To download a video, the app connects directly to the public web pages and video servers of the platform hosting it — the same as opening the link in a browser. Those servers may log standard network information such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. The app is not affiliated with these platforms and provides them no additional data.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: to resolve links and download videos.
  • Write storage (Android 9 and below only): to save videos to the gallery.
  • Accessibility service (optional): see section 3; off by default and enabled only by you.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Content and copyright

Only download content you own or are authorized to save, and follow the terms of the relevant platform and local law. The app is not affiliated with Douyin, TikTok, ByteDance or any video platform.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If advertising or any feature that processes personal data is added in the future, this policy will be updated before that feature is released.
